Paaru asked me...

Do we even have enough moments together?

You asked me whether we've even spent enough moments together for me to feel all this. And honestly, Paaru, we haven't had that many moments, have we?

“Pesamale kaadhal vaaradho.”

But moments, hmm… what say, Paaru? Let's recap???

I can still see you when I close my eyes. I can replay everything in my head. I don't remember much about the first time we met, but I remember so many little things from our second meet. I remember seeing you for the first time that day. You were exiting the hall with one of your friends (I don't remember her ), and you didn't see me, but I recognised you. I remember your expression when I came and talked to you. I remember searching for you in the crowd while the orchestra program was going on. I just wanted to talk to you, that's all. And I remember how you called me to take pictures. You were standing at a distance, waving your hand at me to come and take pictures. That was really cuteee.

I remember asking you to take pictures, but you didn't take any. I remember you sitting there, leaning sideways against the wall. I remember you getting irritated with your saree. I even remember you getting sad because you missed the vade and tea. And then I remember you going in search of tea and vada.

I remember you getting irritated because your sister was late bringing the juice. I remember asking you to show me your mehndi, and you showing me how it was only on one side. I remember you asking me, “Let's take a pic together.”

I remember telling you that I had to leave because of badminton. And I remember you asking me to stay. Honestly, Paaru, I didn't want to leave either.

And you know what else I remember?

I remember looking at you from a distance. You were there in your saree, looking up at the sky, watching the parrots flying around and enjoying them. You looked happy, Paaru. And I saw you there, just enjoying that moment.

I even remember the time I saw you near your home. I remember you walking along the road with your friend, going towards your home. And I remember you waving your hand at me from above the gate. That was kinda cuteeee.

See, Paaru? I remember all these little things. Somehow, all these little things stayed with me.

And out of all of them, our second meet will always be my favourite. I don't know what it was about that day, but there's something about that memory that makes me smile every time I think about it.

Maybe it was just one ordinary day for you, but for me, it became a memory I can go back to whenever I want. I can close my eyes and picture everything all over again, Paaru. The way you looked, the little things you did, the way you smiled, and even those tiny moments you probably never thought I'd remember.

I really can visualise it all when I close my eyes, Paaru. And honestly, that's what makes it so special to me.
